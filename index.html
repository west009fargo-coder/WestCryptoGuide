Security

Protect yourself before you invest.

Knowing what NOT to do is one of the most important crypto skills.

🚨 Never Do This

  • ❌ Share your seed phrase.
  • ❌ Share your private key.
  • ❌ Trust guaranteed profit promises.
  • ❌ Send crypto to strangers promising double returns.
  • ❌ Connect your wallet to suspicious websites.

✅ Always Do This

  • ✓ Verify addresses before sending.
  • ✓ Confirm the blockchain network.
  • ✓ Research projects carefully.
  • ✓ Start small while learning.
  • ✓ Keep recovery information private.
FAQ

Common beginner questions.

Can I lose money in cryptocurrency?

Yes. Crypto prices can be extremely volatile and you can lose some or all of the money you invest.

Do I need to buy one whole Bitcoin?

No. Bitcoin is divisible into very small units called satoshis.

What is a seed phrase?

A seed phrase is a backup used by compatible wallets to regenerate the keys that control your crypto accounts.

Can someone ask for my seed phrase?

A legitimate support person or service should never need your seed phrase to receive or send crypto.
